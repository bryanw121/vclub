Big day: Terms and Privacy pages, faster event and chat screens, a text-size setting, feedback emails, readable Venmo notes and the tournament engine shipped. 15 more PRs are built and waiting on database changes.

## Shipped

**Features**
- Terms and Privacy pages, plus "By continuing… you agree" on sign-up and login (#105)
- Text size setting (Small to Extra large) that scales the whole app on phone and web (#117)
- Feedback form submissions are emailed to the team, at most 5 per hour per user (#118)
- Tournaments: seeded pools, ref duty, result entry, single and double elimination brackets seeded from the pools, standings, podium, and an upcoming/past list (#121)

**Fixes**
- The Venmo Pay note is readable ("Open Play · Sat, Oct 31 · vteam") instead of a `+`-joined slug (#114)
- Scrolling the feed no longer loads event pages nobody opened (#126)
- Chat: you're never stuck at the top of a long conversation, and older messages load one page at a time (#112, #113)

**Under the hood**
- Event page: newest 100 comments with "Load earlier", the edit form opens already filled in, and pages load when you press a card (#104)
- Web chat only renders what's on screen, opens on the newest message, and shows a "new messages" pill (#107)
- Tournament comments are paged; avatars show on the first frame (#110)
- Tests: a coverage report and floor, database access-rule checks against beta, more end-to-end paths, and retries for flaky network steps (#119); the tournament end-to-end test is hardened against races (#127)
